Lumetype is a small, independent project made with love by Rohit Saka. By using it, you agree to these few simple terms.

Using Lumetype

The free plan gives you the full editor, every language and every template, with exports up to 720p carrying a small watermark. Pro costs ₹500 a month and removes the watermark, exports up to 4K and is unlimited.

Paying and cancelling

Pro renews every month through Razorpay until you cancel. You can cancel anytime from your account page: you won't be charged again, and Pro stays on until the end of the month you've already paid for.

Refunds

If you're charged twice, charged after cancelling, or Pro doesn't work because of something on our side, you'll get your money back. Reach out (below) and refunds go back to your original payment method within a week.

Your content

Your videos and everything you make with Lumetype are yours, including for commercial use. We never receive them. Please only caption videos you have the right to use.

Captions are automatic

Captions, timings and translations are made automatically and can contain mistakes. Please give them a look before you publish.
