Privacy

Your videos stay yours.

Last updated 20 October 2026

Lumetype is a small, independent project made with love by Rohit Saka. This page explains, in plain words, what happens to your information when you use it.

Your videos never leave your device

Everything Lumetype does to your video happens inside your browser: trimming, speech recognition, captions, translation and the final export. Your videos, audio, transcripts and captions are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. Your projects are saved in your own browser, so clearing your browser's data deletes them.

What we collect

Nothing, if you just use the studio. If you sign in to go Pro, Google shares your name and email address with us, and we keep those along with whether your subscription is active. That's all.

Payments

Pro is paid through Razorpay. You enter your payment details on Razorpay's own checkout, and we never see or store your card, UPI or bank details.

Cookies

One cookie keeps you signed in, if you sign in. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies.

Files the studio downloads

To work on your device, the studio downloads the speech recognition model from Hugging Face, Google Fonts from jsDelivr when you pick one, and Chrome may fetch translation packs from Google. Like any website, those services see your IP address and what was requested, but never your video, audio or text.

Your choices

You can ask for your account to be deleted at any time, and anything we hold about you goes with it, except records the law requires us to keep for payments.

Changes

If this ever changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions? Reach Rohit Saka on GitHub at github.com/RohitSaka.
