    Questions

    Fair questions,
    straight answers.

    Is Lumetype free to try?

    Yes. The full editor works for free, in every language, with every template. Free exports are 720p with a small watermark. Pro removes the watermark, exports up to 4K and is unlimited for ₹500 a month.

    Is my video uploaded anywhere?

    No. Trimming, speech recognition, captions and the export all happen in your browser, on your own device. Our servers only handle sign-in and your subscription.

    Which languages does it understand?

    99, detected automatically, including Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, Bengali, Marathi and Urdu. When it isn’t certain which language you speak, it asks rather than guessing. Captions can be written as spoken, in Roman letters (Tinglish, Hinglish…) or translated.

    What do I need to run it?

    A recent Chrome, Edge or Safari on a laptop or a modern phone. The first time, your browser downloads the speech model once (about 100 to 600 MB, depending on how accurate you want it) and keeps it for next time.

    How do I pay, and can I cancel?

    Through Razorpay, with UPI Autopay, credit or debit cards (including international cards) or a netbanking mandate. It renews monthly and you can cancel anytime from your account.

    Can I finish my edit in Premiere Pro or DaVinci Resolve?

    Yes. Export subtitles (SRT or VTT), or an edit list that rebuilds your trimmed cut from the original file, ready to open in Premiere Pro or Resolve.

    Does it work with audio only, like a podcast?

    Yes. Drop in an MP3, M4A or WAV and your captions are drawn on a colour of your choice, ready to export as a video or to key over footage in your editor.
